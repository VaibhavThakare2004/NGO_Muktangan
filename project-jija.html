About the project

When a child is born, every family deserves to know.

Project Jija was born from the belief that the first days and weeks of a newborn's life are the most critical — and the most preventable for complications. By equipping parents, grandparents, and caregivers with evidence-based knowledge, we reduce neonatal risk at its root.

Inspired by Dr Abhijeet (paediatrician) and led by Dr Ashwini — who has decades of work in safe motherhood and childhood — Jija brings trusted paediatric guidance into the hands of every family, in every language, in every country.

Our model is peer-to-peer: paediatricians in each country are the educators. They bring their clinical expertise and the trust their communities already place in them — and they lead the conversation with families in their own language and culture.

What we do

Three pillars of newborn wellbeing

🌟

Educate Families

Parents and grandparents receive clear, culturally appropriate guidance on feeding, warmth, hygiene, danger signs, and when to seek care — before the baby arrives and in the first year of life.

🩺

Empower Paediatricians

Paediatricians across 100 countries lead community education in their regions. They adapt Jija's evidence-based curriculum to their language, customs, and health system — and bring their authority as clinicians to every family they reach.

🌍

Build a Global Network

Every participating paediatrician joins a living network — sharing outcomes, translating materials, and growing the evidence base so Jija continuously improves with real-world data.

Global ambition

100 countries. One mission.

Project Jija is designed from the ground up to cross borders. The curriculum is modular, translatable, and adaptable — so a paediatrician in Kenya, Brazil, or Vietnam can deliver the same evidence-based content in their own language and context.
