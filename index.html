HPLC Testing Camp

As a tribute to Dr. Rashid Merchant, we hosted a comprehensive HPLC testing camp at Savitri Jyotirao College of Social Work, Yavatmal, on Teachers' Day This initiative not only paid homage to Dr. Merchant's legacy but also provided 150 students with a unique opportunity to gain practical experience in HPLC testing, enhancing their skills and knowledge in the field.
