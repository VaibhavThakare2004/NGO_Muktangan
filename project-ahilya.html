About the Programme

Named after Ahilyabai Holkar — a beacon of strength.

Project Ahilya takes its name from the 18th-century ruler Ahilyabai Holkar, revered for her compassion, wisdom, and dedication to her people. In the same spirit, this programme gives every adolescent girl the knowledge and confidence to understand her own body — without shame, without fear.


Delivered entirely online through community sessions, peer leaders, and trusted educators, Ahilya reaches girls in schools, community halls, and homes across Maharashtra — at no cost to families or participants.

What We Teach

Three pillars of adolescent wellbeing.

Every session is designed by paediatricians and delivered by trained community leaders — age-appropriate, culturally sensitive, and medically accurate.

🩸

Menstrual Health & Hygiene

Understanding the menstrual cycle, practical hygiene practices, and how to manage periods safely and comfortably — dispelling myths along the way.

💪

Body Awareness & Nutrition

Recognising puberty changes, the importance of nutrition and iron-rich foods, and building a healthy relationship with one's own body during adolescence.

🛡️

Emotional Wellbeing & Safety

Developing self-confidence, understanding personal boundaries, and knowing when and how to seek help from a trusted adult or health professional.

How It Works

Peer-led, paediatrician-designed, community-rooted.

01

Train community leaders

30 trusted women leaders across Maharashtra are trained by Muktangan paediatricians to run Ahilya sessions in their communities.

02

Deliver online sessions

Live and recorded sessions are held on accessible platforms — reachable from a phone, school, or community centre.

03

Engage families

Parents and guardians are invited to join orientation sessions, so the learning continues at home in a supportive environment.

04

Follow up & measure

Leaders check in with participants and track progress toward the 10,000-girl milestone, with paediatrician oversight throughout.
