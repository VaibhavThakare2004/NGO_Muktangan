Your Impact

What your gift does

These are indicative amounts drawn from our field costs. Even the smallest contribution adds to a body of work that has changed thousands of lives.

₹500one gift

Covers one family's health education session — trained health workers visit a household, cover safe pregnancy, nutrition, and early child care.

₹1,000one gift

Sponsors a rural ultrasound — early anomaly detection for a pregnant woman in a village without a diagnostic centre.

₹2,500one gift

Funds a complete follow-up cycle — Hb, height, weight, and complication tracking for one woman from pregnancy to baby's first birthday.

₹5,000one gift

Enables a thalassemia awareness camp — reaching an entire school or panchayat, educating families on carrier screening and prevention.
